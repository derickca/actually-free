# Decision records

One file per decision for this project, kept under the Decision Records Policy (agents-policy, docs/decision-records-policy.md). The policy is the canonical text and governs if anything here differs from it.

Each record is one page at most and carries, in order: the name and status (Proposed until the owner rules, then Ruled, with the date of the ruling); the context, in plain words, with the real current values; the options, each with one concrete case and its good sides and bad sides stated plainly, with anything unresolved written as a NEEDS CLARIFICATION marker; the scope, stating what else was checked; and the recommendation, labelled as the recommendation and last. Before a record is presented, a checker who did not write it reads it as a hostile reviewer and adds the check section at the end of the file. When the owner rules, the record gains the outcome, written as "chosen, because…", and a Consequences section stating at least one cost as plainly as the benefits, and the ruling is entered in DECISIONS.md at the root in the owner's words.
