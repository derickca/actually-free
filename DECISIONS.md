# Decisions

The owner's rulings for this project, in his words. Entries stand under their ruling day, newest first. An entry is added only when the owner rules, and only in the words he ruled in; nothing is entered from a summary or a paraphrase. Each ruling's full record — the question, the options and the check — stands as its own file in docs/decisions/, kept under the Decision Records Policy (agents-policy, docs/decision-records-policy.md).

No entries stand yet. Rulings this project already runs on are cited where they are used, in the code and the docs; each gains its entry here when the owner puts its words on record.
